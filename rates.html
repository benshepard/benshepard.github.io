Rates & Policies

My rate is $90/hour, and I prefer payment via Zelle, check, cash, or Venmo. Payment is due at the conclusion of each session. My rate applies to all the time I spend with you, including time on the ground and in the aircraft—not just the aircraft’s Hobbs time.

Scheduling

Lessons will be arranged directly once availability, aircraft access, and training objectives have been confirmed.

Cancellation and weather

If you need to cancel an appointment, please give me as much notice as possible by email, text, or phone. No-show appointments and appointments canceled with less than 24 hours’ notice will be billed for the full time scheduled.

Training typically takes place regardless of the weather unless we explicitly agree otherwise before the session by text, email, or phone. If conditions are unsuitable for flying, we can often use the scheduled time for ground instruction, required paperwork, or a simulator session.

Preparation and billing

Lesson preparation, ground briefings, flight time, and debriefing expectations will be explained before training begins.

Aircraft and insurance

Aircraft rental, renter insurance, instructor coverage, club or operator requirements, and any required checkouts must be coordinated independently.
