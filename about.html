THE INSTRUCTOR

Flying is personal.
So is teaching.

I love the challenge of learning to fly well. Just as much, I value the chance to help someone else discover what they're capable of.

Meet Ben

I’m a Bay Area pilot with a particular interest in the fundamentals of airmanship: understanding an airplane, handling it precisely, and making sound decisions. My flying has included single-engine, instrument, and tailwheel training, and I’ve begun aerobatic training in a Super Decathlon.

Long before aviation, years working in coffee taught me how much I enjoy serving people. Football taught me something equally important: you don't need to be in the spotlight to make a meaningful contribution. Those lessons shape the kind of instructor I hope to be — someone who prepares thoroughly, listens carefully, and takes genuine pride in a student's progress.

One of the biggest turning points in my own flying was learning from an instructor with decades of experience. He challenged me to revisit the basics and understand them more deeply. I want to carry that same respect for mentorship into every lesson: stay curious, set high standards, and never make asking a question feel uncomfortable.

More than a checkride

I care about standards because they give us something concrete to work toward. But reaching a standard once isn't the finish line. I want students to keep testing what they've learned in a supportive environment, building the consistency and judgment to handle the unexpected. A checkride is an important result of that work, not the reason for it.

My hope is that students finish training proud not only of a certificate, but of how they earned it: by showing up prepared, taking on challenges, learning from setbacks, and becoming more confident in themselves.
