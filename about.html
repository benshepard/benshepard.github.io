Meet Ben

I’m a Bay Area pilot with experience in single-engine flying, instrument operations, and tailwheel aircraft. My background includes the FAA Advanced Ground Instructor (AGI) and Instrument Ground Instructor (IGI) certificates.

I value thoughtful preparation, direct communication, and a practical understanding of why aircraft behave the way they do. My aim is to make each lesson an opportunity to connect knowledge, skill, and judgment.

This site is designed to be both an introduction to my instruction services and a useful reference for students between flights.
