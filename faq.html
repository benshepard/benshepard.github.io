FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Good questions make better pilots.

A straightforward guide to getting started, planning your training, and making the most of every lesson.

How do I begin flight training?

Start with a conversation about your experience, schedule, goals, and where you expect to fly. We can identify an appropriate aircraft or rental arrangement and map out an initial learning plan.

What does a private pilot certificate require?

For an airplane single-engine private pilot certificate under Part 61, the FAA generally specifies at least 40 flight hours, including required dual and solo experience, as well as knowledge, proficiency, eligibility, and testing requirements. Many pilots need more than the minimum. See 14 CFR 61.103–61.109.

How long does training take?

It depends on how frequently you fly, aircraft and instructor availability, weather, and your progress. Consistent lessons and preparation often improve continuity.

Do you supply an airplane?

Aircraft rental and availability are separate from instruction unless specifically arranged. We will discuss a suitable airplane or flight-school/club rental option before scheduling.

Do you offer ground instruction?

Yes, the website is designed around one-on-one ground learning, including regulations, aerodynamics, systems, weather, navigation, and practical-test preparation. Contact me to discuss topics and format.

Can you help with commercial pilot preparation?

Commercial preparation can include maneuver analysis, precision, flight planning, scenario-based decision-making, and knowledge review, subject to confirming instructor privileges and aircraft availability.

What about instrument training?

Instrument ground instruction and study support can be discussed. Any instrument flight instruction must be provided by an instructor holding the applicable flight instructor instrument privileges.

Where are lessons conducted?

The San Francisco Bay Area is the proposed service region. Specific airports and aircraft access will be confirmed before bookings.

What should I bring to a lesson?

A logbook, identification and certificates appropriate to the lesson, a headset if you have one, and relevant study materials. I’ll confirm any additional items before the flight.

How do payment and cancellation policies work?

See Rates & Policies for the planned approach. Final pricing, cancellation windows, payment methods, and any travel charges will be posted before instruction is offered.
